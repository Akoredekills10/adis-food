OUR STORY

More Than Just Food

Adis Foods and Drink is a food, drinks, catering and event-planning business located at Charity Road, Pamada/Abule-Egba, Lagos, Nigeria.

We are passionate about providing delicious meals, refreshing drinks and quality service to our customers. From a quick shawarma after a long day to food and drinks for a special celebration, we are here to make every experience enjoyable.

Our menu includes shawarma, fish, grills, asun, chicken and chips, salad, cocktails, mocktails and other refreshing drinks.
